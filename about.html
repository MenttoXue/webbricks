Our Story

Villa Toscana is a reflection of the Italian table: generous, seasonal, and unhurried.

Founded with a deep respect for regional Italian cooking and Mediterranean ingredients, Villa Toscana offers a refined yet approachable dining experience. We believe that the finest meals arise from simplicity—excellent produce, careful technique, and an atmosphere that invites conversation.

Our kitchen draws inspiration from the traditions of Tuscany and the coastal regions of Italy, while remaining attentive to what is fresh and available locally. Pasta is made in-house daily. Olive oils, cheeses, and cured meats are selected with care. The wine list favors small producers who share our regard for place and season.

Hospitality is central to everything we do. Whether you join us for a quiet midweek supper or a celebration, our team is committed to making your visit considered and memorable.

Seasonal

Menus evolve with the harvest. We prioritize ingredients at their peak and adapt preparations accordingly.

Authentic

Recipes are rooted in Italian and Mediterranean tradition, executed with precision rather than reinvention for its own sake.

Considered

From the table setting to the final espresso, every detail is chosen to support a calm and gracious experience.
